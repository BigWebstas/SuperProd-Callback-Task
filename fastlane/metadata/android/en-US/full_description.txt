Watches for missed calls and turns each one into a "call back" task in
Super Productivity, filtered by your own rules.

* Known vs. unknown caller, and whether to also capture declined calls.
* A foreground service with the call log as the source of truth, so
  nothing is missed if a notification is dropped.
* Tasks are created via Super Productivity's Local REST API, reached
  either over your own LAN or through a reverse proxy you control.
* Queued deliveries retry automatically until they land, with a
  Room-backed dedup ledger so a call is never turned into two tasks.

Requires a Super Productivity desktop instance, always running and
reachable from the phone, with its Local REST API enabled.
